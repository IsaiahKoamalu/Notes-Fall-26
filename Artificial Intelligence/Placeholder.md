## Review of Probability
- Product rule: $p(A|B)=\frac{p(AB)}{p(B)}$
- $p(5 | odd) = \frac {p(5 and odd)}{p(odd)} = \frac{\frac{1}{6}}{\frac{3}{6}} = \frac{1}{3}$

## Marginal Probability
- Prob of a single event ocurring, independent of everything else
	- take the joint probability and marginalize sum
- Joint distribution
	- can have multiple variables
- 

**EX:** Medical test for rare diseases has the following joint probabilities:

|          | $D$    | $\not D$ |
| -------- | ------ | -------- |
| $T$      | $.009$ | $.099$   |
| $\not T$ | $.001$ | $.891$   |
$P(D|T)=\frac{P(T,D)}{P(T)}=\frac{P(T,D)}{P(T,D)+P(T,\not D)}=\frac{0.009}{0.009+0.99}=0.083$
